converted to a split FP32 ONNX encoder and dynamic-prompt decoder (opset 17).
The decoder preserves all four author mask candidates and prior low-resolution
mask-logit inputs. No model retraining or checkpoint weight edits were applied.
Model provenance, exact file sizes and SHA-256 digests accompany the weights in
resources/segmentation/mobilesam/manifest.json. Original research photos,
validation outputs, Python environments and the original checkpoint are not
included in the application package.
