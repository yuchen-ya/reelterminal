# Excalidraw and font notices

Excalidraw 0.18.1 declares MIT in its published package manifest. The npm
archive omits the standalone license; `Excalidraw-MIT.txt` preserves the
upstream copyright and complete license from the 0.18 release source.

The unchanged font binaries come from `@excalidraw/excalidraw@0.18.1/dist/prod/fonts`.
The companion notices preserve the font authors' copyrights and license terms:

- Excalidraw: <https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/blob/v0.18.0/LICENSE>
- Assistant: <https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/assistant/OFL.txt>
- Cascadia Code: <https://github.com/microsoft/cascadia-code/blob/main/LICENSE>
- Comic Shanns: font name-table notice embedded in <https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/blob/v0.18.0/packages/excalidraw/fonts/ComicShanns/index.ts>
- Excalifont: complete OFL and name-table copyright embedded in <https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/blob/v0.18.0/packages/excalidraw/fonts/Excalifont/index.ts>
- Liberation Sans: <https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts/blob/main/LICENSE>
- Lilita One: <https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/lilitaone/OFL.txt>
- Nunito: <https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/nunito/OFL.txt>
- Virgil: <https://github.com/excalidraw/virgil/blob/main/LICENSE.md>
- Xiaolai: <https://github.com/lxgw/kose-font/blob/master/OFL.txt>; name-table copyright in <https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/blob/v0.18.0/packages/excalidraw/fonts/Xiaolai/index.ts>
